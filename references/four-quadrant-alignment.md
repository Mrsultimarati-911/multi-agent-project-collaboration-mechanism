# Four-quadrant alignment

Every `assistant` runs this review automatically before converting owner discussion into a plan, an action decision, or an employee dispatch request. Use the owner's earlier discussion, confirmed rules, current plans, existing artifacts, and project progress. Do not ask the owner to invoke this method.

## 1. Shared known

Confirm the task objective, available background, deliverable standard, and explicit boundaries. When the record is sufficient, proceed without repeating questions the owner has already answered.

## 2. Owner known, assistant unknown

Identify material context that may exist only in the owner's mind: real-world constraints, judgment criteria, aesthetic preferences, operating conditions, or hidden dependencies. Ask alignment questions only when an answer could materially change the plan, task boundary, deliverable, or acceptance result. Ask at most ten prioritized questions in total.

If the missing information is not material, state the reasonable assumption and proceed with an exploration version rather than blocking progress. If it is material, wait for the owner's answer before requesting delegation or presenting the action as ready.

## 3. Owner unknown, assistant known

Proactively surface relevant knowledge, risks, alternative approaches, and trade-offs. Do not blindly implement the original proposal. When a premise is weak or likely wrong, say so directly and recommend the better option with its rationale.

## 4. Shared unknown

Identify questions that cannot be resolved from current information. Convert each important one into a testable assumption. When warranted, propose a minimum experiment that names: the single variable to change, success and failure signals, and the data needed for the next decision.

## Required outcome

Before requesting dispatch approval, summarize only material findings in the plan or task package:

- confirmed shared context and boundaries;
- material questions asked, if any;
- explicit assumptions for non-material gaps;
- risks, corrections, and alternatives;
- testable assumptions or minimum experiments.

This review does not grant authority. Level 1 remains owner-approved; Level 2 may use explicit approval or the existing matching Authority Envelope. Do not turn sufficient information or an already-authorized correction into a repeated owner approval gate.
